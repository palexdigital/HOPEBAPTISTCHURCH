Discover Hope Baptist Church

Hope Baptist Church is situated in Sunnyside, Pretoria, where the congregation gathers using the facilities of the Pretoria Faith Community. From this base, the ministry extends its reach across the city and beyond, building relationships grounded in faith, integrity and service.

Hope Baptist Church (HBC) is committed to promoting the key components of Good Governance, Transparency and Accountability within the Church and civil environments. We believe that effective, durable and sustainable service delivery in Africa can be realised within a practical, God-fearing environment.

Our Heart & Ministry

Hope Baptist Church is situated in Sunnyside, Pretoria, where the congregation gathers using the facilities of the Pretoria Faith Community. From this base, the ministry extends its reach across the city and beyond, building relationships grounded in faith, integrity and service.

Hope Baptist Church (HBC) is committed to promoting the key components of Good Governance, Transparency and Accountability within the Church and civil environments. We believe that effective, durable and sustainable service delivery in Africa can be realised within a practical, God-fearing environment.

VISION & MISSION

The principles that guide our ministry and outreach.

OUR VISION

To reach out with the gospel to civic authorities, and to assist them in fulfilling their mandates with a measure of excellence.

OUR MISSION

To preach the good news of the gospel to civic authorities with a view to building up these vital persons, and to build a support network for them to achieve excellence in performance and service delivery. We are further committed to training religious leaders in excellence in ministry.
